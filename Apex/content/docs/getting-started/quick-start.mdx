This guide walks you from a fresh APEX workspace to your first approved change request.

## Step 1: Sign In

Go to [app.apex.io](https://app.apex.io) and sign in with your credentials. If you're the first person in your organisation, you'll be the **Tenant Admin** by default.

> **Trial accounts:** During your 14-day trial, all Professional features are unlocked. No credit card required.

## Step 2: Invite Your Team

1. Click your avatar in the top-right corner → **User Management**
2. Click **Invite User**
3. Enter the user's email address and select their role
4. They'll receive an invitation email with a link to set their password

See [User Roles](/docs/getting-started/user-roles) for a full breakdown of what each role can do.

## Step 3: Create a Department (Optional)

Departments help organise users and scope visibility. Skip this for now if your team is small.

1. Click your avatar → **Department Management**
2. Click **Add Department**
3. Enter the department name and assign a manager

## Step 4: Submit Your First Change Request

1. Click **Change Requests** in the left sidebar
2. Click **New Change Request**
3. Fill in the required fields:
   - **Title** — a clear, concise summary of the change
   - **Description** — what you're changing and why
   - **Risk Level** — Low, Medium, or High
   - **Scheduled Date** — when you plan to make the change
   - **Rollback Plan** — how you'd undo this if something goes wrong
4. Click **Submit for Review**

The change request is now in `Submitted` status and visible to Managers and Tenant Admins.

## Step 5: Approve the Change Request

If you have the Manager or Tenant Admin role:

1. Click the change request from the list
2. Review the details
3. Click **Approve**

The change request moves to `Approved` status. The submitter receives an in-app notification and email.

## Step 6: Complete the Change

When the change has been implemented:

1. Open the approved change request
2. Click **Mark as Completed**
3. Optionally add completion notes

The change is now in `Completed` status and the full lifecycle is recorded in the audit trail.
